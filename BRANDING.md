# Branding and identity — Rotaract TC-25

This document supplements [LICENSE](./LICENSE). It is informational and does not replace legal advice.

## What this covers

The following are treated as part of the club’s identity and are **not** offered for unrestricted reuse:

- The names **“Rotaract TC-25”**, **“Rotaract Club of Tema Community 25”**, and confusingly similar variants used in connection with this project  
- Logos, marks, short titles (for example **“RT”** as used in the official site navigation), taglines, and distinctive layouts tied to this codebase  
- Imagery, copy, and structured content **specific to this club** as delivered through the CMS or website (subject also to third-party rights in photos or fonts)

## What you must not do

Without **prior written permission** from Rotaract TC-25 (or as otherwise required by Rotary International / Rotaract branding rules where applicable), you must **not**:

1. Use club names, marks, or imagery **for commercial purposes** (including ads, resale, SaaS, client work, or paid templates derived from this site).  
2. Present a product, service, or site **as official Rotaract TC-25** when it is not.  
3. Copy or adapt the site’s branding package to imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with Rotary International, a Rotary district, or Rotaract TC-25 where none exists.

## Rotary / Rotaract context

**Rotary** and **Rotaract** are programs and marks administered by **Rotary International** and related entities. Use of those marks must comply with [Rotary International’s branding guidelines](https://brandcenter.rotary.org/) and applicable policies. Nothing in this repository grants rights to use Rotary’s trademarks beyond what those policies allow.

## Permitted non-commercial reference

You may refer to the club by its correct name in **good faith, non-commercial** contexts (for example factually naming the organization in news or academic discussion), provided you do not imply endorsement and do not reuse proprietary artwork from this repo without permission.

## Questions

For branding approvals, corrections, or licensing requests, use **official Rotaract TC-25 / Rotary channels**, not informal assumptions based on this repository alone.
